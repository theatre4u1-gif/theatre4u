# App store screenshots — shot-list & sizes (all three apps)

The apps are Capacitor shells loading the live site, so "screenshots" = the running app.
Capture logged in, with a tidy demo inventory loaded (use the in-app "Load sample data" to
fill the screen), on a device or simulator at the exact sizes below. You can pre-stage by
opening the site in a desktop browser's device-emulation mode at the phone resolution, but
final store shots are cleanest from the real build on a simulator.

## Required sizes

### Apple App Store (per app)
- iPhone 6.7" — 1290 × 2796 px (portrait). REQUIRED. 3–10 shots.
  (6.9" 1320 × 2868 also accepted; one modern set covers the rest.)
- iPad 13" — 2064 × 2752 px (or 12.9" 2048 × 2732). REQUIRED only if the app supports iPad.
- No separate feature graphic needed for Apple.

### Google Play (per app)
- Phone — min 2, up to 8. Portrait 1080 × 1920 or 1080 × 2400. REQUIRED.
- 7" + 10" tablet shots — only if you list tablet support.
- Feature graphic — 1024 × 500 px. REQUIRED (build from the brand promo assets).
- App icon — 512 × 512 px (already have per brand).

## Suggested shot set — Theatre4u
1. Inventory grid — a full, tidy inventory (costumes/props/sets) with photos. Caption: "Every costume and prop, in one place."
2. Item detail + QR code. Caption: "Every item gets a QR code."
3. Backstage Exchange browse. Caption: "Borrow, lend, rent, and sell with programs near you."
4. Funding Tracker (a source + expenditures). Caption: "Track grants and donations — paperwork builds itself."
5. Reports / inventory value. Caption: "Reports for budgets, grants, and insurance."
6. (optional) Scan-to-join team / QR scan.

## Suggested shot set — ArtsTracker
1. Inventory grid (mixed: instruments/supplies/costumes). Caption: "Everything your program owns, searchable."
2. Item detail + QR code. Caption: "Every item gets a QR code."
3. The Exchange browse. Caption: "Share resources with programs near you."
4. Funding Tracker. Caption: "Track grants and donations automatically."
5. Reports / inventory value. Caption: "Reports for budgets, grants, and insurance."

## Suggested shot set — BinTrove
1. My Bins grid (labeled home items). Caption: "Know what's in every bin."
2. A QR-labeled bin + scan result. Caption: "Scan a bin — see what's inside."
3. Reports / value by category. Caption: "A home inventory for insurance."
4. Add item from photo. Caption: "Snap a photo — it becomes an item."

## Feature graphics (Google, 1024 × 500)
Build one per brand from the promo assets:
- BinTrove — Home-Product/Promo/ (before/after + logo on teal).
- Theatre4u / ArtsTracker — brand logo + a clean inventory shot on the brand color; add tagline.

## Notes
- Keep real names/data generic in screenshots (sample data is fine; avoid real student/customer names).
- Same 5-shot story works across iPhone, iPad, and Android — just re-capture at each required size.
- Marketing text baked onto screenshots (captions above) lifts conversion; optional but recommended.
