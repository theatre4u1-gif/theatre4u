## Full description
Theatre4u is the inventory and resource-sharing app built for the people who make theatre happen — high schools, colleges, and community theatres.

Catalog every costume, prop, lighting fixture, and set piece in one searchable place. Print a QR label for every bin and shelf, then scan with any phone to see exactly what's inside. Stop losing the good scissors, the magic wand, and the costume that walked off after closing night.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Catalog costumes, props, lighting, sound, sets, and more — each item with its own QR code
• Print QR labels for bins and shelves; scan to see contents instantly
• Organize by storage location — scene shop, costume closet, loft
• Backstage Exchange — browse, borrow, rent, lend, and sell with theatre programs across the country
• Funding Tracker — log grants, donations, and allocations; the documentation builds itself
• Reports — inventory value, condition, and location for budgets, grants, insurance, and audits
• Invite your team — cast, crew, and volunteers — each with the right access

BUILT FOR THEATRE PEOPLE
Not a generic inventory app. Built by a theatre person who has lived the chaos of one production rolling into the next.

FREE TO START
Your first 25 items are free — no credit card. Upgrade to Pro any time for unlimited items, full reports, and Backstage Exchange sharing.
