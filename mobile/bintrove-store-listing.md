## Full description
Ever tear apart the garage looking for the Christmas lights? BinTrove makes sure you always know what you own and where it is.

Snap a photo of what's in a bin, stick on a QR label, and scan it anytime to see what's inside — without opening the lid. Catalog the whole house: holiday decorations, the garage, tools, kitchen overflow, keepsakes, kids' things, and seasonal clothing.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Snap a photo and it becomes an inventory item
• Print QR code labels for every bin — scan with any phone to see the contents
• Organize by location: closet, garage, attic, storage unit
• Search everything you own in seconds
• Export a full home inventory (with values and photos) for insurance

MOVING MADE EASY
Label every box as you pack, set each one's destination room, and scan to see what's inside before you cut the tape. Unpack by priority instead of guessing.

FREE TO START
Your first 30 items and 3 bins are free — no credit card. Upgrade any time for more bins and items, or to share with your household.
