## Full description
ArtsTracker is the inventory and resource-sharing app for arts and activity programs — theatre, music, dance, visual arts, and boosters.

Catalog everything your program owns in one searchable place — instruments, costumes, props, supplies, equipment, and gear — each item with its own QR code. Print labels for every bin and shelf and scan with any phone to see what's inside. Know what you have, find what you need, and share with programs near you.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Catalog your inventory by program type — music, theatre, dance, visual arts, booster
• Print QR labels for bins and shelves; scan to see contents instantly
• Organize by storage location — instrument room, costume room, supply closet
• The Exchange — browse, borrow, rent, lend, and sell with programs across the country
• Funding Tracker — log grants, donations, and allocations; the documentation builds itself
• Reports — inventory value, condition, and location for budgets, grants, insurance, and audits
• Invite your team — aides, volunteers, and staff — each with the right access

BUILT FOR ARTS PROGRAMS
Not a generic inventory app. Built for the people who run arts and activity programs — by someone who has lived it.

FREE TO START
Your first 25 items are free — no credit card. Upgrade to Pro any time for unlimited items, full reports, and Exchange sharing.
